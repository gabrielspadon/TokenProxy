## Workflow and measurement rules

Preserve time/provider/account/model scope and exact selection in URL state. Scope must actually reach its endpoint. Summary, inspect and investigate retain their distinct jobs. Every action explains target, effect timing and reversible scope, reports persisted/refused/stale/partial outcomes, and retains nonsecret drafts after errors. Passwords and secrets remain write-only under their existing clearing rules.

Keep quota units, reset horizons, observation age, provider health, local eligibility and entitlement separate. Keep estimates, reported tokens, cache read/write, signed byte changes and recorded cost separate. Preserve unknown historical decomposition, negative savings and zero-usage exclusions. Do not sum completion upserts as attempt totals. Explicit client cache TTL wins. Do not label release metadata as software activation or configuration export as complete database backup.

The capability inventories `redesign-capabilities-*.json` are the parity denominator. Every supported/unexposed or partial capability gets an implemented journey or a source-supported unavailable/internal-only classification. Keep permission and execution evidence beside the row. A route count is not parity evidence.
