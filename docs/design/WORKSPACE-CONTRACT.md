# Shared design contract

Provided by the operator on 2026-09-06. Its measurement, investigation and control semantics remain applicable. The newer [corrective redesign mandate](REDESIGN-20260907.md) supersedes its visual choices, including IBM Plex Sans and indigo selection, with Manrope and petrol-teal. Earlier rejected compositions remain rejected. The original text below is retained as historical intent, not the current visual specification.

Design TokenProxy as a precise analytical workspace for an operator supervising their AI gateway. Make it immediately clear which accounts can accept work, where sessions are routed, what consumes context and quota, and what the recorded usage costs. Organize the main experience around three connected views, Capacity, Context, and Economics. Give each a distinct analytical purpose while preserving the same navigation, scope controls, selection behavior, and inspection pattern. Prioritize the information that explains a constraint, reveals an unexpected outcome, or supports a decision.

Develop the current light workspace into a deliberate visual identity. Use white working surfaces, a cool gray canvas, deep navy navigation, restrained indigo selection, IBM Plex Sans for interface text, and IBM Plex Mono for identifiers and measured values. Create visual distinction through proportion, aligned numbers, shared chart axes, precise typography, and carefully controlled density. The memorable element should be the coordinated relationship between activity, account comparison, and the selected evidence. Provider colors identify providers, metric colors identify quantities, and selection has its own consistent accent. Reserve strong contrast for the active investigation and consequential exceptions.

Make investigation continuous across the workspace. Preserve time range, provider, account, and model filters when moving between Capacity, Context, and Economics. Selecting an account, interval, session, or request should update the relevant charts and detail panel without losing the surrounding comparison. Keep the inspector resizable and the selected record unmistakable. Explain routing through ordered candidates, eligibility, skip reasons, session pinning, and observed fallback decisions whenever those records exist. Show the requested model alongside the model that actually served the request. Place explanations beside the evidence that supports them, with deeper technical details available on demand.

Treat measurement integrity as a design requirement. Show each quota window with its own scope, units, reset time, and observation age, and separate future reset horizons from historical activity. Distinguish provider health, local routing eligibility, and evidence of model support. Keep reported token usage, context estimates, cache reads, cache writes, output, and recorded cost estimates visibly separate. Missing values remain unknown, and a historical zero cost does not establish free usage. A prefix discontinuity does not prove compaction. Bytes removed do not automatically establish token or monetary savings. Every comparison needs an explicit period and denominator, and every claimed saving needs supporting evidence.

Make controls understandable before they are applied. Explain which account, route, session, or request population a change affects, when it takes effect, and how it can be reversed. Distinguish draining from disabling, session continuity from account rotation, and semantic-preserving processing from transformations that remove content. Keep content-changing transformations explicitly opt-in. Distinguish a local configuration check from an authenticated upstream test or a paid inference request. Keep credentials masked, preserve the scope of destructive-action confirmations, and provide clear feedback when a change is saved, refused, stale, or only partially applied.

Work autonomously from the current workspace brief, components, and data contracts. Respect the recorded rejection of earlier compositions. When a layout decision is uncertain, compare a small number of rendered alternatives using representative workload data, then develop the strongest consistently. Inspect account comparison and linked analysis at 1440×1000 and 1920×1080 before extending the composition across all routes. Verify narrow layouts, readable text, keyboard access, reduced motion, persistent filters, chart-to-table selection, and actual control persistence. Keep live updates from moving selected rows or interrupting reading. Preserve the best observed version, refine specific defects, and finish with evidence of visual and behavioral verification plus any remaining limitations.
