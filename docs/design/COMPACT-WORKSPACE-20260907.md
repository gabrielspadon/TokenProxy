# Coherent compact workspace

The operator's correction is about coherent compactness, not smaller components. Keep readable text, recognizable brands and accessible targets. Reduce repeated hierarchy and context switches, and place decisions beside their evidence.

The rendered baseline repeats scope, status and selection controls across multiple bars. A selected record forces an additional full-width banner. Every intermediate layout opens a fullscreen inspector. The account panel calls a row layout Compare, while actual multi-account comparison is elsewhere. Equal-height cards and viewport-driven columns waste space when the inspector changes the available width.

## Chosen composition

One shared scope strip groups the time and population selectors, with saved investigations, retained selection and evidence actions beside them. Retained selection becomes an intrinsic summary control; its exact identity, excluded-scope explanation and clearing actions are available in a focused popover. A selection does not add an obligatory full-width row. Shared scope and selection continue to persist in the URL and across views.

One account region groups search, state facets, display preferences and refresh. Cards and Rows describe presentation. Comparison means selecting up to four accounts and opening the existing evidence-backed comparison. Direct controls keep their existing concurrency checks, persisted readback and conflict handling. Quota summaries put measured percentage, reset and age together. Further detail is an explicit disclosure rather than repeated prose around every measure. Recorded health, configured state and model-specific routing eligibility stay distinct.

Account name, next observed reset and recorded headroom support different decisions. Unknown or stale values cannot win a capacity ranking. Clock ticks do not reorder the account collection. Cards respond to their actual container, including space taken by the inspector, and do not create decorative blank height.

Primary display settings remain directly available in narrow account regions. The operator rejected the proposed View options disclosure because additional layers increase the work of using the application. Checkbox glyphs remain 20px inside larger native label targets.

Freshness for ranking advances with the local receipt of new source data, never with a timestamp supplied by an observation. Historical snapshots retain their own anchor. Selected and compared accounts keep their positions when new measurements arrive. Configured state and derived health labels do not assert that an authenticated probe passed.

The wide inspector preserves simultaneous comparison and evidence. Intermediate widths use a bounded side overlay; phone widths use a full-width detail flow with a clear return. The existing stable portal, drafts, caret and focus restoration remain intact.

Navigation identifies destinations once. Environment evidence lives in the header, observation controls remain in the rail, and search has one persistent entry plus its keyboard shortcut. Icons share one alignment and optical grammar. Provider marks remain recognizable identities, without repeated decorative tiles competing with control surfaces.

## Alternatives considered

Blanket size reduction would preserve the conflicting hierarchy and weaken readability. Hiding everything behind a single settings menu would make available operations harder to discover. The chosen approach keeps primary decisions visible and reveals exact evidence and secondary actions in context.
